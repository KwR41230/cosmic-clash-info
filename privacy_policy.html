Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Cosmic Clash mobile application for iOS, developed by Kevin Rasmussen.

1. Information Collection

Cosmic Clash is built as a premium, privacy-first game. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account registration required, and the game does not track your location or usage habits.

2. Data Storage

All of your game progress, including earned coins, fleet unlocks, and high scores, is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

3. Apple Game Center

The app uses Apple's Game Center for global leaderboards and achievements. This service is provided by Apple and is governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy. Your scores are only shared with Game Center if you are signed into your Apple ID.

4. No Ads & No In-App Purchases

Cosmic Clash contains no third-party advertisements and no "pay-to-win" in-app purchases. Your gameplay experience is completely uninterrupted.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page.
